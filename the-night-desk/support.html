Reporting a problem

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The game version, if available.
  • What you did just before the issue, and what you expected to happen.
  • Whether the issue happened in portrait or landscape.
  • An optional screenshot with personal information removed.

Missing progress or an ad reward?

Tell us what happened and whether the app closed, lost its connection, or showed an error. Avoid deleting or reinstalling the game while troubleshooting, since progress is stored locally. Never send your Apple or Google password.

Release availability

The game is in development. There is no public App Store download or paid subscription available from this site. Release information will appear on the game page.

Privacy questions

Use the same support address for privacy questions. Our privacy information explains the current website and development-build data practices.
