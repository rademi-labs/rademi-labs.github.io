Privacy information.

Last updated October 20, 2026

The Night Desk is in development. This page describes this website and the current game integration. Advertising and privacy features are still being validated before a public game release; this page will be updated to reflect the released build.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies, account registration, or a contact form. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses, to host and secure the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Game progress

The current game stores progress, settings, and reward records on your device. It does not require a player account or send your game save to a Rademi Labs game server. Device backup settings may separately affect copies of local data.

Advertising in the game

The game integrates Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads. Current development testing uses test ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location, device identifiers, crash and performance information, ad-view data, and interactions for advertising, analytics, and SDK operation. The information processed depends on configuration, available permissions, and privacy choices.

Read Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s SDK data disclosures for Google’s practices. Not watching a rewarded ad is not a promise that an initialized advertising SDK processes no information.

Privacy choices

The release integration must support applicable consent messages and a way to revisit privacy choices where required. That work is not yet complete. The current build does not request Apple tracking authorization; this does not mean that the SDK processes no data. Advertising will remain in development test mode until the release checks are complete.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your address and the message or attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond and investigate the issue. Our support mailbox is provided by Google. Please do not send passwords, payment information, or unrelated personal information.

Questions and requests

To ask about information you have sent us, or request access, correction, or deletion of your support correspondence, contact rademilabsllc@gmail.com. Do not uninstall the game just to contact support: removing an app may remove its local progress. Google and GitHub handle data they process according to their respective policies and controls.

Before public release

The final game’s audience, regional privacy choices, retention details, and App Store data disclosures are still being reviewed. This development notice does not claim that those release requirements have been completed.
