Privacy information.

Last updated October 20, 2026

This page describes how The Night Desk, its optional advertising and purchases, this website, and our support mailbox handle information. Contact Rademi Labs LLC at rademilabsllc@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Your game progress

The game stores progress, settings, and reward records locally on your device. It does not require a player account or upload your game save to a Rademi Labs game server. Your device backup settings may separately affect copies of local data. Removing the app may remove local progress; please contact support before reinstalling to troubleshoot a problem.

The optional playtest log is off unless you enable it in Settings. It records game events locally, is size-limited, and is not automatically uploaded. Copy Diagnostics lets you copy a report and choose whether to send it to support.

Optional advertising

The game uses Google AdMob to offer optional rewarded videos. You can play without watching them. The integration disables ad personalization and Google's publisher first-party identifier and does not request Apple's tracking authorization.

These settings do not mean that advertising processes no data. Google's Mobile Ads and consent SDKs may process IP addresses and approximate location, device identifiers, ad-view and interaction information, and crash, performance, and diagnostic information. This supports ad delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, service operation, and privacy choices. The information available depends on the device, permissions, configuration, and your choices. The SDK may initialize or load an ad before you choose to watch a video.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosures for Google's processing practices and controls.

Your advertising choices

Google's consent system checks which privacy messages apply before the game requests ads. When a choice is required, follow the message shown in the game. Where Google requires a way to revisit your choices, Settings displays a Privacy Options button. The game checks permission again after you use that button. You can always choose not to watch rewarded videos.

Optional purchases

Apple handles payment for the one-time Video-free Rewards unlock. The game uses StoreKit to check verified purchase status and restore access, including changes such as refunds. Rademi Labs does not receive your payment-card details through the game. The game does not upload StoreKit transaction details to a Rademi Labs server.

The unlock lets you manually claim eligible optional rewards without watching videos; it does not guarantee that the advertising SDK is never initialized. Resetting game progress does not cancel an Apple purchase. Use Restore Purchases in the Optional Store with the same Apple account. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's privacy information.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies, account registration, or a contact form. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses, to host and secure the site. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your address and the message or attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond and investigate your request. Our support mailbox is provided by Google. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or unrelated personal information.

Questions, access and deletion requests

Contact rademilabsllc@gmail.com to ask about information you have sent us or request access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence. Tell us which correspondence your request concerns without including unnecessary sensitive information. Rights and any retention obligations depend on applicable law. Google, Apple, and GitHub manage information they process under their respective policies; use their privacy controls for requests concerning their services.

We may update this page when the game's features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
